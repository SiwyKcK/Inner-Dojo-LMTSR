Inner Dojo integration and this review were prepared for SiwyKcK, with AI-assisted
development. Original LMTSR, Resurrection-derived training assets, Lone Wolf,
Sekiro and supporting tool/library assets retain their respective authorship.
This document does not grant or invent permission on behalf of those authors.

- Lone Wolf Master (MinNuez): https://www.nexusmods.com/sekiro/mods/2400
- Smithbox / Andre.SoulsFormats: https://github.com/vawser/Smithbox
- SoulsFormatsNEXT: https://github.com/soulsmods/SoulsFormatsNEXT
- Paramdex: https://github.com/soulsmods/Paramdex

Exact source filenames, versions and modification provenance are retained in
`SOURCE_ORIGINS.json` and each variant's `DOCS/SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json`.
The schema snapshot is unmodified external Paramdex data. Tool binaries and
proprietary Oodle are deliberately external prerequisites, not claimed as our code.
No blanket license is applied here to inherited game/mod assets. Review access
does not relicense them.

`source_assets` are identified binary donor/copy assets. Original upstream
authoring projects for inherited Lua/UI/navigation/game assets are not available
and are not represented as newly authored source. The supplied source covers
our integration workflow, event program representation and asset/delta processing.

Original build scripts and evidence were extracted from the exact published
release ZIPs. Review adapters were added on 6 November 2026 without changing
those releases. Retain the historical source copies when comparing provenance.
