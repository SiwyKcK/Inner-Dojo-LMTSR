# Uninstall / rollback

Close the game. Prefer your full pre-installation backup.

DOCS/ROLLBACK/mods restores the exact before-overlay files from original NOOB plus original LoneWolf main/fixes. That stage has the author's complete Hardcore PARAM and is not a validated NOOB-compatible playable state. Do not launch at that intermediate stage; restore original NOOB or a known compatible backup before playing.

Only remove these added Dojo overrides when they did not exist before installation:

- mods/event/m10_00_50_91.emevd.dcx
- mods/map/m10_00_00_00/m10_00_00_00.nva.dcx
- mods/map/m10_00_00_00/m10_00_00_00.nvmhktbnd.dcx
- mods/menu/02_205_bossselect.gfx
- mods/menu/hi/menu_boss_11490.tpf.dcx

Do not remove the complete mods directory, author assets, game saves or unrelated files. INSTALL_MANIFEST.json lists precise paths/hashes. The restore set is not a personalized-installation backup.
